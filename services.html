Straight answers to what clients ask us most.

How quickly can you fill an open role?

For most roles, we deliver a shortlist within 5–10 business days thanks to our pre-screened talent pipeline across 15+ industries.

Yes — we support permanent hires, contract staffing, and remote placements, all run through the same compliance-first process.

Our relationship doesn't end at the offer letter. We stay engaged through onboarding, payroll setup, and ongoing HR support.
