Questions

Practical answers.

What is the minimum engagement size?

There isn't one. We've filled single roles for five-person firms and run multi-site staffing for companies with several hundred people on our payroll.

How are recruitment fees structured?

A percentage of annual CTC for contingency hiring, with a replacement guarantee period. Retained search is staged across the mandate. Managed payroll is a per-head monthly charge. Everything is agreed in writing before we start.

Can you take over payroll from an existing provider?

Yes, and we do it regularly. Transitions are planned around a month-end so no salary cycle is disrupted.

Do you handle PF, ESI and labour law filings?

Yes — provident fund, ESI, professional tax, gratuity, contract labour registers and returns are all part of the compliance service.

Which locations can you staff?

Strongest across the Gurgaon–Dharuhera–Rewari corridor and the wider NCR, with delivery elsewhere in India through our network, plus global HR support for UK and Singapore operations.

Tell us what you need done.

A short conversation is usually enough to tell you whether we're the right firm for the job — and we'll say so if we're not.
