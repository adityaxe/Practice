Leadership

Four decades of professional practice behind every mandate.

Synergy Plus Business Consulting Group was founded on 1 April 2014 across Gurgaon and Dharuhera, with a straightforward premise: the firms in India's industrial corridor deserved the same calibre of advice that large corporates buy from the big consultancies.

Our founder qualified as both a Chartered Accountant and a Company Secretary in 1977–78 after graduating from Shri Ram College of Commerce, and has since served as Secretary cum Financial Controller, Chief Executive and Director for businesses in India, the United Kingdom and Singapore. That bench of experience is what sits behind our consulting work — and why our recruitment desks understand what a finance or operations leader is actually being hired to fix.

To provide top professionals to the corporate world, and to help candidates realise their true potential.

Questions

Before you get in touch.

How quickly can you deliver a shortlist?

For most mid-level and plant roles we come back within about a week. Senior and specialist mandates take longer because we map the market properly rather than forwarding whatever the job boards return.

Do candidates pay anything?

No. Candidates are never charged at any stage. Our fees are paid by the hiring organisation, which is the standard and the only ethical arrangement.

Can you run payroll without doing our recruitment?

Yes. Payroll management, statutory compliance, background verification and accounting are all available as standalone engagements.

What does "corporate services" actually cover?

Facilities-side work delivered from our Dharuhera company — electrical audits, engineering, AMC and repair, project management, security, housekeeping, pest control and travel. Plants often take these alongside staffing.

Do you work outside India?

We do. Our leadership has held executive roles in the United Kingdom and Singapore, and we support global HR mandates for clients operating across those markets.

Let's talk about the work.

Tell us whether you're building a team or looking for your next role. You'll speak to a consultant, not a form-filling queue.

Brief us on the role.

Send the spec — or just the problem you're trying to solve. We'll come back with a view on the market, the salary band and a timeline.

Send us your CV.

We'll review it properly, tell you honestly where you stand, and match you against live mandates. No fee, no spam.
