Leadership

The practitioner behind the practice.

Most consultancies sell you the partner and deliver the analyst. We are built the other way round. Our founder qualified as both a Chartered Accountant with the ICAI and a Company Secretary with the ICSI in 1977–78, after graduating from Shri Ram College of Commerce — and then spent the following four decades actually running businesses rather than advising from the outside.

That career included service as Secretary cum Financial Controller, as Chief Executive, and as a Director, with mandates in India, the United Kingdom and Singapore. It is the reason our consulting engagements start with the balance sheet and the shop floor rather than a template, and the reason our recruitment desks can tell the difference between a CV that reads well and a candidate who can do the job.

Excellence is not just a skill, it is an attitude. We build partnerships that last a lifetime.

The group was founded on 1 April 2014 with two locations chosen deliberately: Gurgaon, for corporate clients and professional hiring, and Dharuhera, to sit inside the manufacturing belt we serve. A decade on, both are still open nine hours a day, and clients from the first year are still with us.

How we operate

Four commitments we actually hold to.

01

Honest shortlists

We would rather send three candidates we believe in than twelve that pad the numbers. If we can't fill it, we say so early.

02

Never charge candidates

Not for registration, not for placement, not for anything. Fees come from the employer. There are no exceptions.

03

Compliance before convenience

Statutory filings are done on time and correctly, even where a shortcut would be quicker and nobody would notice for a year.

04

Principal-level attention

The person who takes your brief is the person accountable for delivering it. Mandates are not handed down a chain.
