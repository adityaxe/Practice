Founder & CEO

Mr. MD Vanjani

Founded on April 1st, 2014, in the bustling industrial hubs of Millennium City Gurgaon and Dharuhera, Synergy Plus Business Consulting Group was born from a vision of excellence. Mr. Vanjani, a distinguished SRCC alumnus, brings unparalleled expertise holding professional qualifications of Chartered Accountancy (ICAI) and Company Secretary (ICSI) since 1977-78.

He has held senior positions as Secretary cum Financial Controller, CEO, and Director in renowned companies both in India and overseas (UK & Singapore). With over four decades of global experience, he has successfully navigated diverse sectors as a hardcore professional.

"Our power of choice is untrammelled and nothing prevents us from doing what we like the best way, with pleasure."
