Before you write

Quick answers.

How soon will I hear back?

Within one working day for both employers and candidates. If your enquiry arrives over a weekend, expect a reply on Monday.

I'm a candidate — will you charge me?

No. Candidates are never charged at any stage, for any reason. Our fees are paid by hiring organisations.

Can I just call instead?

Of course. Both offices are open nine hours a day — the Gurgaon and Dharuhera numbers are listed alongside this form.

What happens to my CV?

It is held confidentially and only shared with an employer once we have spoken to you and you have agreed to that specific application.
