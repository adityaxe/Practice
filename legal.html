Privacy policy

Synergy Plus Business Consulting Group ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you use this website or engage our services, why we collect it, and what rights you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Identity and contact data — name, email address, telephone number and postal address.
  • Candidate data — your CV, employment history, qualifications, current and expected remuneration, notice period and references.
  • Client data — company name, role briefs, contractual and billing details.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type and version, and basic usage information.

2. How we use it

We process personal data only where we have a lawful basis to do so, specifically:

  • To match candidates with suitable roles and to present candidates to prospective employers, with consent for each specific application.
  • To perform a contract we have entered into with a client or candidate, including payroll and compliance services.
  • To meet statutory and regulatory obligations, including tax, provident fund, ESI and labour law requirements.
  • For our legitimate interests in operating and improving our services, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.

3. Sharing your information

A candidate's CV is shared with a prospective employer only after we have spoken with the candidate and they have agreed to that particular application. We also share data with statutory authorities where the law requires it, and with service providers who process data on our instructions under confidentiality obligations. We do not sell personal data.

4. Retention

Candidate records are retained while they remain relevant to our placement services and for as long as statutory record-keeping requires. You may ask us to delete your data at any time, subject to any records we are legally obliged to keep.

5. Your rights

You may request access to the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccuracies, request erasure, or object to particular processing. Write to mdvanjani@udyogservice.in and we will respond within a reasonable period.

6. Fees

We never charge candidates a fee of any kind, at any stage. If anyone claiming to represent us asks you for payment in connection with a job, do not pay, and please report it to us immediately.
